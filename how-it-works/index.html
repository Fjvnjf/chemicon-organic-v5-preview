THE WORK BEHIND THE BOX

Good food, with a record behind it.

The public experience stays calm because the operating work is connected underneath. These are the checkpoints that shape what can be offered and delivered.

01

FIELD CONNECTION

Start with the field.

Student field representatives connect farmer clusters to the operating team and record crop, harvest and checklist context.

Context is recorded close to the source
02

EVIDENCE FIRST

Check before listing.

Residue status is recorded against each lot. Passed lots may enter the catalog; pending and failed lots stay out.

Only passed lots are offered publicly
03

CAPACITY WITH CARE

Reserve what can move.

A preorder is tied to an approved offer, delivery zone and available capacity. When a cycle fills, the waitlist keeps the request visible.

Availability follows the live cycle
04

THE HANDOFF

Finish at your door.

Confirmed orders move into a zone manifest for packing and rider handoff, with delivery status kept alongside the order.

Dhaka pilot zones are the current focus
